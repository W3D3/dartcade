## 3. Camera stills

If your board has a camera, the bridge sends still images to the backend so you can see your
throw. These stills are held in memory only, for the current session, and are never written to
disk or kept in the database.

## 4. Cookies

Dartcade uses exactly one cookie: a session cookie that keeps you signed in. There's no analytics
and no tracking of any kind.

## 5. Who else sees data

- **Our mail provider** sends account mail (sign-up confirmation, password reset) on our behalf,
  as a processor. It's currently Resend, Inc., based in the USA; mail content may be transferred
  there under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **Hosting**: Dartcade runs on the operator's own server, at home, in Austria. No other hosting
  provider is involved.

Nobody else receives your data. It isn't sold, rented or used for advertising.

## 6. How long

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists.

## 7. After you delete your account

Deleting your account (Settings → Account) removes your account and personal data. Your name
stays visible in the match history of games you played with others — those records belong to the
other players too, and a deleted account's name there no longer links back to a user.
